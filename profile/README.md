## Open source for research

The Eisenberg Family Depression Center publishes open source tools, automations, and research workflows designed to **accelerate mental health discoveries while remaining highly reusable across any research discipline**. By reducing technical barriers and promoting interdisciplinary collaboration, we help researchers solve complex data problems without starting from zero.

### What we build

- **Research software** — Tools and reusable workflows that accelerate research and reproducibility.
- **Data & visualization** — Practical approaches to cleaning, analyzing, visualizing, and reporting research data.
- **Automation** — Solutions that reduce repetitive technical work and help research teams move faster.
- **Mobile technologies** — Resources for wearables, sensors, apps, and mobile data used in research.

## Get involved

Open source works best when students, developers, and researchers can learn from one another and build on shared solutions.

### Students

Work with real research software and learn from existing projects. Contributions can include code, documentation, testing, design, examples, or new ideas.

### Developers

Fork something useful. Improve it. Report bugs. Contribute solutions that can help research teams well beyond your own.

### Researchers

Use our tools in your work, adapt them to your studies, tell us what's missing, or collaborate with us on new solutions.
